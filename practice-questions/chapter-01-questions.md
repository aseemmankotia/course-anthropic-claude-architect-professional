## Chapter 1: The Senior-Architect Remit: CCAR-P Exam Overview — Practice Questions

**Q1.** A candidate answers 55 of 63 questions correctly on the CCAR-P exam but fails. Which statement BEST explains how this is possible?

A) The exam has a minimum number of questions that must be answered within the time limit, and the candidate ran out of time
B) Raw correct-answer counts are converted to a scaled score (100-1000) that weights domains unevenly, so missing questions in heavily-weighted domains can fail a candidate despite a high raw count
C) Pearson VUE applies a random penalty to scores at test centers but not on OnVUE
D) The candidate must have answered fewer than 63 questions, which automatically triggers a failing scaled score

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: B**

CCAR-P reports a scaled score of 100-1000 with 720 as the passing threshold; scaling reflects domain weighting, so raw percentage correct does not map linearly to pass/fail.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Time limits affect completion, not scaled scoring conversion — this is a plausible but irrelevant distractor
- There is no documented difference in scoring methodology between OnVUE and test-center delivery
- The scenario states all 63 questions were answered; this option contradicts the premise

> ⚠️ Frequently missed on the exam — read the options carefully.

</details>

**Q2.** A CCAR-P credential holder's certification is set to expire in 11 months. They complete all renewal requirements 3 weeks before the expiration date. What is the outcome?

A) They must pay a late renewal fee because renewal was not completed at least 6 months in advance
B) The credential lapses regardless of timing and a full retake is required
C) The 12-month validity period resets only after Anthropic manually reviews the application, which can take up to a year
D) The renewal is processed at no cost because it was completed on time, before the 12-month validity window closed

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: D**

CCAR-P credentials are valid for 12 months and renewal completed on time (before expiration) is free.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- No documented fee structure penalizes renewals completed within the 12-month window, regardless of how close to expiration
- On-time renewal prevents lapse by definition — this contradicts the stated policy
- There is no manual year-long review process for standard on-time renewals

</details>

**Q6.** Candidate A answers the most questions correctly overall but concentrates errors in the governance and stakeholder domains. Candidate B answers fewer total questions correctly but distributes correct answers evenly, including strong performance in governance and stakeholder domains. Who is more likely to pass?

A) Candidate A, because total raw correct answers determine the scaled score
B) Both candidates receive identical scaled scores since Pearson VUE scores all domains equally
C) Candidate B, because scaled scoring weights domains unevenly and governance/stakeholder domains carry significant combined weight
D) Neither can pass without a perfect score in every domain

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: C**

Because scaled scoring reflects domain weighting rather than raw totals, strong performance in heavily-weighted domains like governance and stakeholder management matters more than total correct count.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Raw count alone does not determine the scaled score — this is the core misconception the scaled model corrects for
- Domains are explicitly weighted unevenly, not scored equally
- A passing scaled score of 720 does not require perfection in every domain

> ⚠️ Frequently missed on the exam — read the options carefully.

</details>

**Q7.** What primarily distinguishes the CCAR-P judgment bar from a Foundations-level certification?

A) CCAR-P focuses on memorizing exact API syntax and command flags
B) CCAR-P is entirely multiple-select, while Foundations is entirely true/false
C) CCAR-P evaluates a candidate's ability to defend and justify architectural trade-off decisions, not recall syntax
D) CCAR-P has no time limit, allowing deeper syntax-level analysis

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: C**

The Professional-level judgment bar tests defense and justification of architectural decisions under realistic constraints, distinguishing it from lower-level recall-based testing.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Syntax recall is explicitly described as not the focus of Professional-level testing
- CCAR-P questions are single-answer, four-option format, not multiple-select or true/false
- CCAR-P has a fixed 120-minute time limit

</details>

**Q8.** Through which platform must a candidate register for the CCAR-P exam?

A) Directly through Pearson VUE's general certification marketplace
B) A third-party exam aggregator site
C) The Anthropic Partner Academy portal
D) Email request to Anthropic support

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

**Correct: C**

Candidates register for CCAR-P through the Anthropic Partner Academy portal, which then schedules delivery via Pearson VUE.

**Why the others are wrong:**
- Pearson VUE delivers the exam but registration is initiated through the Partner Academy portal, not Pearson's general marketplace
- No third-party aggregator is part of the official registration path
- Registration is not handled via direct support email

</details>
