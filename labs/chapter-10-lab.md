# Chapter 10 Lab: Developer Productivity and Operational Enablement

**Estimated time:** 30 minutes

## Exercise

Design an internal MCP catalog structure and a shared CLAUDE.md template for a 200-engineer organization, plus an incident runbook for an agent producing incorrect tool calls in production

## Key takeaway

Operational enablement — shared standards, catalogs, and runbooks — is the lightest-weighted domain but directly determines integration quality at scale.
