# Chapter 5 Lab: Solution Design II: Resilience, Scaling, and Reference Architectures

**Estimated time:** 35 minutes

## Exercise

Design a resilient reference architecture for a customer-facing support assistant that must survive a regional outage and a model-provider rate-limit event, including a fallback tier and caching layers

## Key takeaway

Enterprise-grade architecture requires explicit resilience and scaling design, not just a working happy-path integration.
