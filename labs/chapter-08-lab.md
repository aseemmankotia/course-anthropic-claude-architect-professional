# Chapter 8 Lab: Stakeholder Communication and Lifecycle Management

**Estimated time:** 35 minutes

## Exercise

Write a one-page stakeholder decision memo defending a chosen architecture (model tier, agent pattern, integration approach) against two alternatives, tailored for both a CTO and a compliance lead

## Key takeaway

Senior architects are judged on their ability to justify decisions to stakeholders and plan for change over the system's lifecycle, not just initial build quality.
