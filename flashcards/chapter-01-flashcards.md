## Chapter 1: The Senior-Architect Remit: CCAR-P Exam Overview — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | CCAR-P exam: how many questions and how much time? | 63 questions, 120 minutes. Exam tip: average ~1.9 minutes per question — budget time, don't dwell on judgment-heavy scenarios early. |
| 2 | CCAR-P scaled score range and passing threshold? | Scaled 100-1000; passing score is 720. Exam tip: raw percent correct ≠ scaled score because domains are weighted unevenly. |
| 3 | CCAR-P credential validity period? | 12 months. Exam tip: renewal completed on time (before expiration) is free. |
| 4 | CCAR-P recommended (not required) experience thresholds? | 3+ years architecture experience, 6+ months production Claude experience. Exam tip: these are recommendations, not registration prerequisites — no formal prerequisite exists. |
| 5 | Combined weight of governance, stakeholder, and developer-enablement domains? | ~35% combined. Exam tip: this is the single largest combined domain block — under-preparing here disproportionately risks the 720 threshold. |
| 6 | CCAR-P vs Foundations-level: what's the core judgment difference? | Foundations tests recall/syntax; CCAR-P (Professional) tests defense and justification of architectural trade-off decisions under constraints. |
| 7 | Raw percentage correct vs scaled score — which determines pass/fail? | Scaled score (720 threshold), not raw percentage. Domain weighting means missing heavily-weighted domain questions can fail a high raw-percentage candidate. |
| 8 | On-time renewal vs late renewal for CCAR-P? | On-time renewal (before the 12-month expiration) is free. Exam tip: treat the 12-month window as a hard deadline, not a soft guideline. |
| 9 | Where do you register for the CCAR-P exam? | Anthropic Partner Academy portal. Delivery happens via Pearson VUE OnVUE (remote) or an in-person test center. |
| 10 | Two delivery modes for CCAR-P exam? | Pearson VUE OnVUE (remote proctored) or physical Pearson VUE test center. Exam tip: both use the same question bank and scoring model — no difference in difficulty. |
| 11 | Why does CCAR-P avoid testing exact syntax recall? | Professional-level certification validates judgment: the ability to weigh trade-offs and defend architectural decisions to stakeholders, not memorize command flags. |
| 12 | Exam question weighting pattern: why can two candidates with equal raw scores get different outcomes? | Because each question's contribution to the scaled score depends on its domain weight — concentrating correct answers in low-weight domains while missing high-weight domain questions (e.g., governance, stakeholder) can produce a lower scaled score. |
